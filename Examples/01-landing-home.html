The Hierarchy

Capital cascades through four tiers of accountability.

Every reserve balance narrows in scope as it moves down the chain — broad at the top, personal at the bottom, transparent throughout.

Tier 1 · Global World Bank

Global reserve custody, proof-of-reserve attestation, and national bank registration.

Tier 2 · Country National Bank

Capital allocation to local branches, rate policy, and reserve ratio settings.

Tier 3 · Branch Local Bank

Loan approval, KYC review, and day-to-day community lending decisions.

Tier 4 · You Client

Borrow, save, and build credit — collateral-based or credit-based.
