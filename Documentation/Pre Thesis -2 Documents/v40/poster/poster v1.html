Development finance moves through layered institutions — World Bank, national and local development banks — but settlement is slow, reserves are opaque, and idle capital sits in pre-funded correspondent accounts.

Flat DeFi protocols solve part of this at scale — Aave v3 alone holds $26.3B TVL — but ignore institutional hierarchy entirely:

  • One pool for all users — no tiers, no role-based governance
  • No cross-tier capital flow between banks and clients
  • No behavioral fraud detection or explainable credit scoring
  • No on-chain group / solidarity lending for the unbanked
02
Objectives
  1. Specify a four-tier lending architecture on an EVM chain, testnet-deployed end to end.
  2. Extend beyond pool lending — deposits, savings, solidarity group loans.
  3. Add an off-chain ML layer for fraud & anomaly detection with explainability.
  4. Enforce programmable borrowing limits and role-based access via smart contracts.
  5. Deploy and evaluate on Polygon zkEVM Cardona / Ethereum Sepolia testnets.

Conclusion

CWB specifies a tier-based on-chain banking system that mirrors real institutional finance — World Bank, national and local banks, and clients — instead of the flat single-pool model used by existing DeFi. Combining programmable hierarchy with an AI/ML risk layer and optional conversational agent is, to our knowledge, not addressed together by any prior protocol.

Future work: Polygon zkEVM mainnet-track deployment, InterBankLendingPool & upward deposits, multisig World Bank governance, zkKYC, and Certora formal verification.
