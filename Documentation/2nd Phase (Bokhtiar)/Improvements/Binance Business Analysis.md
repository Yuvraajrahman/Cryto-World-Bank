### 3.2 Fee Mechanics — How the Cut Works

```
EXAMPLE: Retail User buys $10,000 worth of BTC (Market order, Taker)
│
├── Standard fee: 0.10% × $10,000 = $10.00 charged to buyer
│
├── If paid in BNB (25% discount): $10 × 0.75 = $7.50
│
├── Of the $10 fee collected:
│   ├── $9.00  →  Binance operating revenue
│   └── $1.00  →  SAFU fund (10% of all trading fees)
│
└── Seller (Maker, limit order) pays same 0.10% on their side
     Total Binance revenue per $10,000 round trip: ~$20 (both sides)

EXAMPLE: Institutional VIP 5, $100M trade
├── Maker fee: 0.02% × $100M = $20,000
├── Annual volume × maker rate ≈ much of Binance's revenue
└── Institutional accounts ~50–60% of total trading volume
```

## 4. BNB Tokenomics & Gas Economics

### 4.1 BNB Supply Journey

```
BNB SUPPLY MECHANICS
│
├── INITIAL SUPPLY (2017): 200,000,000 BNB
│   ├── ICO investors:  50% = 100M BNB
│   ├── Team:          40% =  80M BNB
│   └── Angel seed:    10% =  20M BNB
│
├── BURN TARGET: 100,000,000 BNB  (50% of original supply)
│
├── CURRENT SUPPLY (Q1 2026): ~136.36M BNB
│   └── Burned to date: ~65M+ BNB  (~$58.5B cumulative value)
│
└── BURN MECHANISMS (3 types):
    │
    ├── 1. Quarterly Auto-Burn (BEP-95 extension)
    │   ├── Formula: B = N × 1000 / (P + K)
    │   │   ├── N = blocks produced in quarter (~3s/block)
    │   │   ├── P = average BNB price during quarter
    │   │   └── K = constant
    │   ├── Q1 2026 burn: 1.37M BNB ($1.277B)
    │   └── Higher price → fewer tokens burned (inverse)
    │
    ├── 2. BEP-95 Real-Time Gas Fee Burns
    │   ├── 30% of all BSC gas fees burned per block
    │   ├── Q2 2025: $450M total gas × 30% = $135M burned
    │   └── Fires every ~3 seconds automatically
    │
    └── 3. Pioneer Burn Program
        └── Recovers lost/unusable BNB → quarterly burn
```

### 4.2 Gas Price Mechanism on BSC

```
BSC GAS ECONOMICS
│
├── Gas model: EIP-1559-style (dynamic base fee + priority tip)
│
├── Gas price: ~1–5 Gwei  (far cheaper than Ethereum's 10–200+ Gwei)
│
├── Typical tx cost:
│   ├── Simple transfer:    $0.05–$0.20
│   ├── Token swap (DeFi):  $0.10–$0.50
│   └── NFT mint:           $0.30–$1.00
│
├── Who sets gas?
│   ├── Network validators set minimum via governance
│   ├── Users bid above base fee via priority tip
│   └── Block producers (validators) collect tip + base fee
│       └── 30% of base fee destroyed (BEP-95 burn)
│
├── Gas revenue Q2 2025: $450M total
│   ├── 70% → validators (reward for block production)
│   └── 30% → burned (deflationary pressure)
│
└── Economic impact of low gas:
    ├── DeFi TVL on BSC: $7.9B (2025, +23.4% YoY)
    ├── Daily transactions ATH: 31M (Oct 2025)
    ├── 4,200+ dApps deployed on BSC
    └── Attracts users priced out of Ethereum
```

## 9. How Users Buy, Sell, Exchange & Fund

### 9.1 Complete User Money Flow

```
USER JOURNEY: MONEY IN → TRADE → MONEY OUT
│
├── STEP 1: FUND YOUR ACCOUNT
│   │
│   ├── Crypto Deposit (FREE — no Binance fee)
│   │   ├── Send crypto from external wallet
│   │   ├── Binance monitors blockchain for inbound tx
│   │   ├── Confirmations required (e.g., 1 for ETH, 2 for BTC)
│   │   └── Balance credited after AML check
│   │
│   ├── Fiat Deposit
│   │   ├── Bank Transfer (SEPA: free, SWIFT: ~$15)
│   │   ├── Credit/Debit Card (1.8–4% fee)
│   │   ├── P2P Marketplace (buy from other users, 0% maker)
│   │   └── Binance Pay (receive from other users)
│   │
│   └── Convert (buy crypto with stored fiat)
│
├── STEP 2: BUY / SELL / EXCHANGE
│   │
│   ├── Spot Trading  (own the actual asset)
│   │   ├── Limit order: set price, wait for fill (maker rate)
│   │   └── Market order: instant fill (taker rate)
│   │
│   ├── Convert  (instant swap, spread-based, no fee label)
│   │   └── Spread: 0.1–0.3% built into exchange rate
│   │
│   ├── P2P  (user-to-user, Binance escrows)
│   │   ├── Maker: 0% fee
│   │   └── Taker: small fee
│   │
│   ├── Margin Trading  (borrowed funds, up to 10x)
│   │   ├── Trading fee + hourly interest
│   │   └── Liquidation risk if collateral drops
│   │
│   ├── Futures / Perpetuals  (derivatives, no expiry)
│   │   ├── Maker: 0.02% / Taker: 0.05%
│   │   └── Funding rate: paid between longs and shorts every 8h
│   │
│   └── Options  (right to buy/sell at strike price)
│       ├── Maker: 0.03% / Taker: 0.03%
│       └── Exercise fee if in-the-money
│
├── STEP 3: EARN (optional)
│   ├── Flexible Savings: park stablecoins at variable APY
│   ├── Locked Staking: BNB/ETH/SOL, fixed term, higher APY
│   ├── Launchpool: stake BNB/FDUSD → earn new tokens
│   └── Dual Investment: structured product with risk
│
└── STEP 4: WITHDRAW
    │
    ├── Crypto Withdrawal
    │   ├── Choose coin + destination address + network
    │   ├── 2FA required
    │   ├── AML screening (Chainalysis)
    │   ├── Binance deducts network fee
    │   └── Broadcast to blockchain
    │
    └── Fiat Withdrawal
        ├── Bank transfer (SEPA / SWIFT / local rails)
        ├── Binance Card (Visa card linked to account)
        └── P2P sell (sell to another user for fiat)
```
