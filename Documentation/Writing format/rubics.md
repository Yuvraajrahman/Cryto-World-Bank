### Chapter 1 — Problem Formulation (CO1)

**Assessment:** Pre-Thesis 1 Report  
**Marks:** 5 total — P1: 2.5 | Supervisor: 2.5  
**Comment:** 2.5 from Chapter 1 of P1

| Section | Content expected |
|---------|------------------|
| **1.1 Background** | Context and domain background for the problem |
| **1.2 Rationale of the Study or Motivation** | Why the problem matters; gap or opportunity |
| **1.2 Problem Statement** | Clear, scoped statement of the engineering/computing problem |
| **1.3 Objective** | Objectives aligned with the problem |
| **1.4 Methodology in Brief** | High-level approach to solving the problem |
| **1.5 Scopes and Challenges** | Boundaries, limitations, and known difficulties |

**Teaching–learning strategy**
- Review of literature and background study
- Prepare problem statement with scope and justification

**Evaluator focus**
- Problem is modern, complex, and field-relevant
- Scope and justification are explicit
- Chapter 1 quality at P1 submission drives the 2.5 P1 marks

### Chapter 2 — Literature Review (CO9)

**Assessment:** Pre-Thesis 1 Report + Final Thesis Report  
**Marks:** 5 total — P1: 2.5 | Supervisor: 2.5  
**Comment:** 2.5 from Chapter 1.5 of P1; 2.5 from Supervisor Marks

| Section | Content expected |
|---------|------------------|
| **2.1 Preliminaries** | Foundations, terminology, and concepts needed for the review |
| **2.2 Review of Existing Research** | Survey of prior work, methods, and tools |
| **2.3 Summary of Key Findings** | Synthesis, gaps, and link to your problem |

**Teaching–learning strategy**
- Perform literature survey and other means to find materials, information and sources relevant to the selected problem
- Comprehend the materials and information obtained and establish the relationship with the problem to be solved

**Evaluator focus**
- Independent research depth, not only textbook summaries
- Clear connection between literature and your design/research choices
- P1 portion may be assessed via **§1.5** (early literature trail) per rubric comments; full Chapter 2 assessed at final report

### Chapter 4 — Design Alternatives (CO5)

**Assessment:** Pre-Thesis 2 Report  
**Marks:** 15 total — P2: 5 | Defense Panel: 10  
**Comment:** 5 from Chapter 4 of P2; 10 from Defense Panel

| Section | Content expected |
|---------|------------------|
| **4.1 Design Process or Methodology Overview** | Design process aligned with objectives, specs, requirements, and constraints |
| **4.2 Preliminary Design or Design (Model) Specification** | Multiple alternative solutions with enough detail to compare |

**Teaching–learning strategy**
- Preparing design process as per objectives, specifications, requirements and constraints
- Preliminary design multiple alternative solutions of the system
- Perform simulation of the alternative design solutions for functional verification

**Evaluator focus**
- At least two meaningful alternative designs
- Simulation or functional verification evidence for alternatives
- P2 Chapter 4 quality = 5 marks; defense panel judges design depth = 10 marks

### Chapter 5 — Evaluation, Analysis, and Final Design (CO6, CO7)

**Assessment:** Final Thesis Report  
**Marks:** CO6 = 10 (Panel: 5, Supervisor: 5) | CO7 = 5 (Supervisor: 5)

#### CO6 — Analysis and Selection (10 marks)

| Section | Content expected |
|---------|------------------|
| **5.1 Performance Evaluation** | Metrics, benchmarks, and test setup |
| **5.2 Analysis of Design Solutions** | Structured comparison of alternatives |
| **Final Design Adjustment** | Changes driven by evaluation (may sit within §5.2 or as its own subsection) |
| **5.3 Statistical Analysis** | Statistical treatment of results where applicable |
| **5.4 Comparisons and Relationships** | Cross-metric comparisons and relationships |
| **5.6 Discussion** | Interpretation, limitations, and implications |

**Teaching–learning strategy**
- Perform analysis of alternative design solutions to obtained the best suitable solution
- Perform optimization of the selected design solution

**Factors to address in analysis**
Cost, efficiency, usability, manufacturability, impact, sustainability, maintainability, and performance — relative to specifications, prerequisites, and benchmarks.

**Comment:** 5 from Defense Panel; 5 from Supervisor Marks (Evaluating Final Report)

---

#### CO7 — Final Design and Development (5 marks)

| Section | Content expected |
|---------|------------------|
| **5.2 Final Design Adjustments** | Documented final design after performance evaluation; implemented solution matches final design |

**Teaching–learning strategy**
- Obtain the final design by make necessary adjustment to the selected design as per performance evaluation result
- Develop the solution as per final design

**Comment:** 5 from Supervisor Marks
