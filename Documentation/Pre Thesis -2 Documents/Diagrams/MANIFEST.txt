Figure bundle for Pre-thesis v30 (Diagrams/)
==========================================
Sources: .svg (Ch* hand diagrams) and .mmd (fig-* mermaid, in Old Diagrams tree).
LaTeX includes generated .pdf.

Rebuild all PDFs:
  ./Diagrams/build-pdfs.sh

Or manually:
  mmdc -i fig-foo.mmd -o Diagrams/fig-foo.pdf -b white --pdfFit   # mermaid
  rsvg-convert -w 1200 -f pdf -o Diagrams/Ch1_foo.pdf Diagrams/Ch1_foo.svg

Compile (from this directory):
  pdflatex Pre-thesis_v30_final.tex && pdflatex Pre-thesis_v30_final.tex
